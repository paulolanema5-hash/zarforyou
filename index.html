Correct. Once your application is reviewed and you are approved, the R10,000 is yours to keep. You will not receive any repayment demands.

All major South African banks are accepted, including Capitec, FNB, Absa, Standard Bank, Nedbank, TymeBank, African Bank, and Discovery Bank.

Most applicants receive a response within 24–72 hours. Many have reported receiving the funds the very next business day.

No. The application is completely free. You will never be asked to pay anything upfront.
